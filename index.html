Age of leaders

A recurring theme among world leaders. But Trump and Putin are not the oldest men leading their nations. How exceptional is it to hold the highest office when well over the retirement age?

    About the data

    Data on birthdates is based on the analysis of Pew Research Center. Their analysis focuses mostly on heads of government as defined by a country’s political system or constitution. They excluded six UN member states because their leader’s exact birth date could not be determined: Afghanistan, Guinea-Bissau, Madagascar, Niger, Sao Tome and Principe, and Somalia. They also excluded San Marino, which is led by two captains regent who share power equally. To learn more about their methodology, click here.

    Years in power are calculated from the start of the current tenure to today's date. Where a leader had earlier tenures, the total is shown in brackets.

    Data provided by Pew Research Center is based on the ages of the leaders as of June 14, 2026. I have updated this dataset as of 2nd November 2026 using regional news articles of the following countries: Serbia, XXXX. All ages and current heads of states are thus as of 2nd November 2026.

    Regime types come from the V-Dem regime classification. For readability purposes I have combined their disctinction between an electoral and liberal democracy into one category 'democracy', though I awknowledge important details are lost due to this choice.
